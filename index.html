Modules

  • Kiosk UI: thin client, no stored secrets, offline queue marked offline.
  • Identity and enrolment: HR enrols; templates in an HSM/KMS-wrapped vault; no raw images kept beyond verification.
  • Attendance: append-only events with idempotency keys and a server-side clock.
  • Visitor and appointment: state machine with timers computed from stored check-in time.
  • Occupancy reconciliation: derives status from evidence, source and age.
  • Notification and escalation: SMS/email/push with delivery and acknowledgement receipts.
  • Admin dashboard, AuthN/AuthZ/audit (OIDC, MFA for privileged roles, server-side RBAC, hash-chained log).
  • Biometric adapter: vendor-neutral interface (enrol, verify, liveness score); measured error rates required before go-live.
  • Data: PostgreSQL, WAL archiving, encrypted backups.

Workflows

  • Arrival: code, active check, face and liveness, optional voice, record only on success, greet by name. Uncertain or failed: manual verification by staff. Three failures lock the code and raise an alert.
  • Visit: scheduled, checked in (timer from actual check-in), warning at T-5, expired, overdue with departure unconfirmed, alert, acknowledged, then extended with reason, checked out, or resolved with documented evidence. Expiry never implies departure.
  • Reconciliation: confirmed present needs door/sensor evidence under 15 min old; clock-in only is last recorded present; clock-out or exit event is departure confirmed; anything else is unknown or discrepancy.

Deployment, privacy, recovery

  • POPIA: complete a privacy impact assessment and lawful-basis decision first; biometrics are special personal information, so document necessity and less intrusive alternatives; offer a non-biometric path; notices, retention schedule, access/correction, incident and complaint procedures; legal review of any consent assumptions.
  • Security: TLS 1.2+, mTLS for kiosks, secrets in a vault, rate limits, step-up MFA, least-privilege RBAC, pen-test before launch.
  • Reliability: daily encrypted backups, tested restores, RPO 15 min / RTO 1 h targets, paper roll-call and manual sign-in during outages, offline records flagged.
  • Hardware: liveness-capable camera, door-controller API, UPS, tamper switch. Pilot to measure false accept and false reject rates before relying on any biometric.
